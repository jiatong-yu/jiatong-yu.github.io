## About {#about}

I'm a researcher working on large language models. My interests lie broadly in understanding learning algorithms and model behavior. I recently obtained my master's degree from Princeton Computer Science, advised by Prof. [Sanjeev Arora.](https://profsanjeevarora.github.io/) Before that, I graduated from Princeton class of 2024, where I had the fortune to work with Prof. [Danqi Chen](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/~danqic/) and Prof. [Kai Li.](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/~li/) 

I'm currently building an auto-research system at a hedge fund in NYC.

I can be reached at [jiatongy [at] princeton.edu](mailto:jiatongy@princeton.edu).
